HOW TO USE BIG BOSS AI

  1. Select the exact instrument and chart timeframe.
  2. Choose sample data to test the interface, or paste at least 27 candle closing prices from a source you trust.
  3. Tap Run Technical Analysis. RSI, EMA and MACD are calculated locally.
  4. Read the explanation. Mixed indicators produce NO CLEAR SETUP.
  5. Save your observation in the journal and review results in demo practice.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

This is a technical-indicator calculator, not a predictive AI model. It does not connect to Quotex, read Quotex account data, fetch live prices, or place trades. Screenshot upload is preview-only. Sample data is synthetic and must never be treated as a live signal.

Binary options are exceptionally high risk and may result in losing the entire stake. No indicator combination guarantees direction or profit. Do not rely on this app for financial decisions. Check local regulations and the platform's authorisation before use.
